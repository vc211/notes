# Research Tools

**Summary**: Notes on tools for literature search, open science and licensing.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [TOPS Open Science 101 (NASA)](https://openscience101.org/dashboard): NASA Transform to Open Science (TOPS) free training on open science practices: sharing data, code and results. *Keywords*: open science, NASA, training, open data, reproducibility
- [Choose a License: Licenses](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/): Side-by-side guide to common open-source licenses and what each permits or requires. *Keywords*: open source, licenses, MIT, GPL, Apache
- [Litmaps](https://app.litmaps.co/): Literature review tool to find papers, map how studies connect and get alerts for new publications. *Keywords*: literature review, citation map, research, papers
- [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com/): Tool that builds a visual graph of papers related to a given paper to help explore a research field. *Keywords*: literature search, citation graph, research, papers
