# Climate Change

**Summary**: Notes on climate change, extreme weather, and natural hazards such as floods and extreme heat. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Urban_Planning]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [The FutureCrop Challenge (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/the-future-crop-challenge/): Kaggle competition on predicting crop yields under future climate conditions with machine learning. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[Machine_Learning]]. *Keywords*: crop yield, climate change, Kaggle, competition, prediction
- [Nature-based Solutions for Disaster and Climate Resilience (SDG Academy, edX)](https://learning.edx.org/course/course-v1:SDGAcademyX+NBS001+3T2023a/block-v1:SDGAcademyX+NBS001+3T2023a+type@sequential+block@9dd4f68b2c134b428bd5f60): Online course from SDG Academy on using ecosystems (nature-based solutions) to reduce disaster risk and adapt to climate change. See [[Urban_Planning]]. *Keywords*: nature-based solutions, disaster risk, adaptation, resilience, course
- [Explainer: Nine 'tipping points' that could be triggered by climate change (Carbon Brief)](https://www.carbonbrief.org/explainer-nine-tipping-points-that-could-be-triggered-by-climate-change/): Explains nine Earth-system tipping points, including AMOC shutdown, ice sheet collapse, Amazon dieback, permafrost thaw, coral die-off and monsoon shifts. *Keywords*: tipping points, AMOC, ice sheets, Amazon, permafrost, monsoon
- [CPC - Climate Weather Linkage: El Niño Southern Oscillation (NOAA)](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/precip/CWlink/MJO/enso.shtml): NOAA Climate Prediction Center hub for ENSO monitoring: current conditions, outlooks, past episodes, impacts and fact sheets. *Keywords*: ENSO, El Niño, La Niña, NOAA, climate monitoring
- [Climate model details (Bureau of Meteorology)](http://www.bom.gov.au/climate/model-summary/model-summary-table.shtml): Australian Bureau of Meteorology table summarizing the international climate models used in its ENSO and climate outlooks. *Keywords*: climate models, ENSO outlook, BOM, seasonal forecast
- [Soil Carbon (Springer, Progress in Soil Science)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-04084-4): Springer book (2014) collecting research on soil carbon: measurement, mapping, dynamics and management. Main topic: [[Soil]]. *Keywords*: soil carbon, soil organic carbon, book, carbon sequestration
- [Global Surface UHI Explorer (Yale)](https://yceo.users.earthengine.app/view/uhimap): Earth Engine app to explore surface urban heat island intensity for cities worldwide. Main topic: [[Urban_Planning]]. See also [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: urban heat island, surface temperature, cities, Earth Engine app
- [Satellite land surface temperature is not air temperature (LinkedIn post, TC Chakraborty)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): Argues that satellite LST (the "skin" temperature of roofs, roads and tree canopies) is far hotter than the air people feel, so using it to show heat hazard misleads the public and overstates how much trees cool the air. Replies discuss limited urban weather stations and when LST is still useful as a proxy. See [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Urban_Planning]]. *Keywords*: land surface temperature, extreme heat, urban heat island, air temperature, science communication
- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): Dataset of about 2.6 million historical flood events extracted from news articles across 150+ countries, published as one Parquet file (CC BY 4.0). See [[Data]]. *Keywords*: floods, natural hazards, dataset, news, Parquet
