# Forests

**Summary**: Notes on forests, deforestation monitoring and forest definitions. Related: [[Carbon_Markets]], [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC) - openMRV](https://www.openmrv.org/web/guest/w/modules/mrv/modules_2/continuous-change-detection-and-classification-ccdc): openMRV training module on CCDC, a time-series method for detecting and classifying land cover change, used for forest monitoring (MRV). Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. See also [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: CCDC, change detection, time series, land cover, MRV
- [Whisp App (Open Foris)](https://whisp.openforis.org/): FAO Open Foris tool that checks plots of land for deforestation risk using many satellite-based layers, to support zero-deforestation rules such as the EU EUDR. See [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: deforestation, EUDR, due diligence, Open Foris, FAO
- [Methodology Breakdown: Verra vs. Gold Standard Reforestation Methodologies (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TK3TDw4bvjg): Video comparing the reforestation carbon credit methodologies of Verra and Gold Standard. Main topic: [[Carbon_Markets]]. *Keywords*: carbon credits, reforestation, Verra, Gold Standard, methodology
- [An open-access database of nature-based carbon offset project boundaries (Research Square)](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-4535931/v1): Preprint presenting an open database of the mapped boundaries of nature-based carbon offset projects. Main topic: [[Carbon_Markets]]. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords*: carbon offsets, project boundaries, nature-based solutions, open data
- [Forest Definitions Applied in Reporting (US Forest Service)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/programs/fia/forestdefinitions): Compares how FIA, RPA and FAO define forest, woodland, other wooded land and timberland, and where each definition is used. *Keywords*: forest definition, FAO, FIA, reporting, woodland
