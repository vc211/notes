# Water Resources

**Summary**: Notes on surface water, rivers and water monitoring. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [EOWater](https://github.com/ShiruiH/EOWater): Earth Engine tool that builds water surface area time series for user-drawn water bodies from Sentinel-2 and Landsat, with cloud masking, CSV outputs and plots. See [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: surface water, time series, Sentinel-2, Landsat, reservoirs
- [SWT-India: India's changing rivers](https://sites.google.com/view/surface-water-trends-india): Open project showing trends in seasonal surface water area of India's rivers from 1991 to 2020, by basin and river reach. See [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: surface water, rivers, India, trends, basins
