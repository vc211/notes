# Deep Learning

**Summary**: Notes on deep learning models, including foundation models for Earth observation. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Vision Foundation Models in Remote Sensing: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.03464): Survey paper (Lu et al., 2024) reviewing remote sensing foundation models by architecture, pre-training data and method, with performance comparisons and open challenges. Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: foundation models, survey, remote sensing, pre-training, computer vision
- [EarthNets](https://earthnets.github.io/): Deep learning platform for Earth observation with data loaders, benchmark datasets and results, pre-trained models, tutorials and a DOFA-CLIP demo. Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: Earth observation, deep learning, benchmarks, datasets, pre-trained models
- [AIforGood 2025 - segment-geospatial workshop](https://samgeo.gishub.org/workshops/AIforGood_2025/): Hands-on workshop on segmenting satellite and aerial imagery with SAMGeo (Segment Anything): point/box/text prompts, time series and cleaning outputs. Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. See also [[Python]]. *Keywords*: SAMGeo, Segment Anything, segmentation, workshop, building footprints
