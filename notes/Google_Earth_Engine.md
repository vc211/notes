# Google Earth Engine

**Summary**: Notes on Google Earth Engine courses, apps and scripts. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC) - openMRV](https://www.openmrv.org/web/guest/w/modules/mrv/modules_2/continuous-change-detection-and-classification-ccdc): openMRV training module on CCDC, a time-series method for detecting and classifying land cover change, used for forest monitoring (MRV). Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. See also [[Forests]]. *Keywords*: CCDC, change detection, time series, land cover, MRV
- [End-to-End Google Earth Engine (Spatial Thoughts)](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/end-to-end-gee.html): Full course on applied remote sensing in Earth Engine: basics, classification, change detection, apps and the Python API, with guided projects. Video lectures: [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLppGmFLhQ1HJuIb7qMiKIv11HiEQhy3ha). Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. See also [[Python]]. *Keywords*: Google Earth Engine, course, classification, change detection, Python API
- [Whisp App (Open Foris)](https://whisp.openforis.org/): FAO Open Foris tool that checks plots of land for deforestation risk using many satellite-based layers, to support zero-deforestation rules such as the EU EUDR. Main topic: [[Forests]]. See also [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: deforestation, EUDR, due diligence, Open Foris, FAO
- [gee_s1_ard: Sentinel-1 analysis-ready data in Earth Engine](https://github.com/adugnag/gee_s1_ard): Repo that builds analysis-ready Sentinel-1 SAR data in Earth Engine: border noise removal, speckle filtering and terrain normalization, in JavaScript and Python. See its [speckle filter script](https://github.com/adugnag/gee_s1_ard/blob/main/javascript/speckle_filter.js). Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: Sentinel-1, SAR, speckle filter, analysis-ready data, terrain correction
- [EOWater](https://github.com/ShiruiH/EOWater): Earth Engine tool that builds water surface area time series for user-drawn water bodies from Sentinel-2 and Landsat, with cloud masking, CSV outputs and plots. Main topic: [[Water_Resources]]. See also [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: surface water, time series, Sentinel-2, Landsat, reservoirs
- [Cloud-Based Remote Sensing with Google Earth Engine: Fundamentals and Applications (EEFA book)](https://www.eefabook.org/go-to-the-book.html): Free Springer open-access book on Earth Engine: programming, image interpretation, time series and applications on land and water. Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: Google Earth Engine, book, open access, time series, applications
- [Global Surface UHI Explorer (Yale)](https://yceo.users.earthengine.app/view/uhimap): Earth Engine app to explore surface urban heat island intensity for cities worldwide. Main topic: [[Urban_Planning]]. See also [[Climate_Change]]. *Keywords*: urban heat island, surface temperature, cities, Earth Engine app
