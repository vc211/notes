# Remote Sensing

**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial imagery, Earth observation, and the tools and models built on them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Course: QGIS for Precision Agriculture (OCWGIS)](https://courses.gisopencourseware.org/course/view.php?id=56): Free course on using QGIS for precision farming: open data, zonal statistics, field data collection with Mergin Maps and drone image processing with WebODM. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[GIS]]. *Keywords*: QGIS, precision agriculture, drones, WebODM, course
- [Continuous Change Detection and Classification (CCDC) - openMRV](https://www.openmrv.org/web/guest/w/modules/mrv/modules_2/continuous-change-detection-and-classification-ccdc): openMRV training module on CCDC, a time-series method for detecting and classifying land cover change, used for forest monitoring (MRV). See [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Forests]]. *Keywords*: CCDC, change detection, time series, land cover, MRV
- [Global bare soil, photosynthetic and non-photosynthetic vegetation fraction, annual at 500 m](https://zenodo.org/records/12071317): Zenodo dataset of yearly global maps at 500 m showing the share of bare soil, green vegetation and dry (non-photosynthetic) vegetation per pixel. Main topic: [[Soil]]. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords*: fractional cover, bare soil, vegetation, global dataset, MODIS
- [End-to-End Google Earth Engine (Spatial Thoughts)](https://courses.spatialthoughts.com/end-to-end-gee.html): Full course on applied remote sensing in Earth Engine: basics, classification, change detection, apps and the Python API, with guided projects. Video lectures: [YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLppGmFLhQ1HJuIb7qMiKIv11HiEQhy3ha). See [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Python]]. *Keywords*: Google Earth Engine, course, classification, change detection, Python API
- [Historical time-series reconstruction benchmark dataset of Landsat bi-monthly aggregates](https://zenodo.org/records/11150343): Zenodo benchmark dataset of Landsat bi-monthly composites for testing methods that fill gaps and rebuild historical image time series. See [[Data]], [[Machine_Learning]]. *Keywords*: Landsat, gap filling, time series, benchmark, reconstruction
- [Whisp App (Open Foris)](https://whisp.openforis.org/): FAO Open Foris tool that checks plots of land for deforestation risk using many satellite-based layers, to support zero-deforestation rules such as the EU EUDR. Main topic: [[Forests]]. See also [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: deforestation, EUDR, due diligence, Open Foris, FAO
- [How to access in-situ reference data via the WorldCereal RDM](https://esa-worldcereal.org/en/how-access-situ-reference-data-worldcereal-rdm): Guide to ESA WorldCereal's Reference Data Module for finding and downloading harmonized ground-truth land cover and crop type data, including through its API. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords*: WorldCereal, reference data, crop type, ground truth, ESA
- [Vision Foundation Models in Remote Sensing: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.03464): Survey paper (Lu et al., 2024) reviewing remote sensing foundation models by architecture, pre-training data and method, with performance comparisons and open challenges. See [[Deep_Learning]]. *Keywords*: foundation models, survey, remote sensing, pre-training, computer vision
- [gee_s1_ard: Sentinel-1 analysis-ready data in Earth Engine](https://github.com/adugnag/gee_s1_ard): Repo that builds analysis-ready Sentinel-1 SAR data in Earth Engine: border noise removal, speckle filtering and terrain normalization, in JavaScript and Python. See its [speckle filter script](https://github.com/adugnag/gee_s1_ard/blob/main/javascript/speckle_filter.js). See [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: Sentinel-1, SAR, speckle filter, analysis-ready data, terrain correction
- [EOWater](https://github.com/ShiruiH/EOWater): Earth Engine tool that builds water surface area time series for user-drawn water bodies from Sentinel-2 and Landsat, with cloud masking, CSV outputs and plots. Main topic: [[Water_Resources]]. See also [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: surface water, time series, Sentinel-2, Landsat, reservoirs
- [EarthNets](https://earthnets.github.io/): Deep learning platform for Earth observation with data loaders, benchmark datasets and results, pre-trained models, tutorials and a DOFA-CLIP demo. See [[Deep_Learning]]. *Keywords*: Earth observation, deep learning, benchmarks, datasets, pre-trained models
- [Top trends driving the adoption of Earth Observation data through 2023 and beyond (Geoawesomeness)](https://geoawesomeness.com/eo-hub/top-trends-driving-the-adoption-of-earth-observation-data-through-2023-and-beyond/): Article on the main trends pushing wider use of Earth observation data. *Keywords*: Earth observation, industry trends, satellite data, adoption
- [Spatialization of Crop Growth Simulation Model Using Remote Sensing (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-981-15-6864-0_4): Book chapter on running crop growth simulation models over space by feeding them remote sensing data. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: crop models, remote sensing, spatial simulation, yield, book chapter
- [NDVI from First Principles (Streambatch)](https://www.streambatch.io/knowledge/ndvi-from-first-principles): Explainer that builds up NDVI from the basics of how plants reflect red and near-infrared light. See [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: NDVI, vegetation index, reflectance, near-infrared
- [Beyond NDVI: What are vegetation indices, and how are they used in precision farming? (Geoawesomeness)](https://geoawesomeness.com/eo-hub/beyond-ndvi-what-are-vegetation-indices-and-how-are-they-used-in-precision-farming/): Article on vegetation indices other than NDVI and how they are used in precision farming. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: vegetation indices, NDVI, precision farming, crop monitoring
- [APIs | Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/analyse/apis): Overview of Copernicus APIs: catalogues (OData, STAC), openEO and Sentinel Hub for processing, OGC map services and on-demand processing. See [[Data]]. *Keywords*: Copernicus, Sentinel, STAC, openEO, Sentinel Hub, API
- [Cloud-Based Remote Sensing with Google Earth Engine: Fundamentals and Applications (EEFA book)](https://www.eefabook.org/go-to-the-book.html): Free Springer open-access book on Earth Engine: programming, image interpretation, time series and applications on land and water. See [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: Google Earth Engine, book, open access, time series, applications
- [InGARSS 2023: All Tutorials and ESA Workshop Materials (Google Doc)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fYKvTUaj_6r6kiwuPrcPyACF1vMQLpHw/edit): Google Doc collecting tutorial and ESA workshop materials from the InGARSS 2023 conference. *Keywords*: InGARSS, ESA, tutorials, workshop, conference
- [LISS4 Image Processing using XArray and Dask (Spatial Thoughts)](https://spatialthoughts.com/2023/12/25/liss4-processing-xarray/): Tutorial: download ISRO LISS-4 imagery from Bhoonidhi and use XArray, rioxarray and Dask to convert it to TOA reflectance as a Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF. See [[Python]]. *Keywords*: LISS-4, ISRO, XArray, Dask, reflectance, COG
- [SWT-India: India's changing rivers](https://sites.google.com/view/surface-water-trends-india): Open project showing trends in seasonal surface water area of India's rivers from 1991 to 2020, by basin and river reach. Main topic: [[Water_Resources]]. *Keywords*: surface water, rivers, India, trends, basins
- [Spectral Characteristics Viewer (USGS Landsat)](https://landsat.usgs.gov/spectral-characteristics-viewer): USGS tool to compare satellite sensor bands against spectra of minerals, vegetation and water to choose the best bands. *Keywords*: spectral bands, Landsat, sensor comparison, reflectance, USGS
- [AIforGood 2025 - segment-geospatial workshop](https://samgeo.gishub.org/workshops/AIforGood_2025/): Hands-on workshop on segmenting satellite and aerial imagery with SAMGeo (Segment Anything): point/box/text prompts, time series and cleaning outputs. See [[Deep_Learning]], [[Python]]. *Keywords*: SAMGeo, Segment Anything, segmentation, workshop, building footprints
- [Planet Tanager open data: Agriculture (STAC)](https://www.planet.com/data/stac/browser/tanager-core-imagery/agriculture/collection.json): Planet open-data STAC collection of Tanager hyperspectral imagery over agricultural areas. See [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: hyperspectral, Tanager, Planet, STAC, open data
- [Global Fields of The World (FTW) dataset (Source Cooperative)](https://source.coop/ftw/global-data): Global 10 m farm field boundaries: about 1.6 billion polygons per year for 2024-2025 in 195 countries, as GeoParquet, PMTiles and Zarr (CC-BY-4.0). Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords*: field boundaries, global dataset, GeoParquet, Sentinel-2, Fields of The World
- [Cloud-Optimized Geospatial Formats Guide](https://guide.cloudnativegeo.org/): Guide to cloud-optimized geospatial formats (COG, Zarr, cloud HDF/NetCDF, COPC, GeoParquet, FlatGeobuf, PMTiles) and how to make and test them. Main topic: [[Data]]. *Keywords*: cloud-native, COG, Zarr, GeoParquet, PMTiles, formats
- [Cloud Native Remote Sensing with Python (Spatial Thoughts, August 2026)](https://spatialthoughts.com/training/python_remote_sensing_20260804/): Spatial Thoughts training page for its August 2026 class on cloud-native remote sensing with Python (materials behind a passphrase). See [[Python]]. *Keywords*: cloud-native, remote sensing, Python, training, Spatial Thoughts
- [NISAR Polarimetric Analysis Training Toolkit](https://github.com/dharmendraisro/nisar-polarimetric-training): Python/Jupyter training for NISAR L2 GCOV radar data: finding, subsetting, QC, visualization, GIS export and polarimetric decomposition. See [[Python]]. *Keywords*: NISAR, SAR, polarimetry, Jupyter, radar
- [crop-stage-detection (NASA Harvest)](https://github.com/nasaharvest/crop-stage-detection): Crop-agnostic model that estimates a field's growth stage from an NDVI time series (Sentinel-2/Landsat via Earth Engine), no crop calibration needed. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: crop phenology, growth stage, NDVI, NASA Harvest, time series
- [crop-cycle-detection (NASA Harvest)](https://github.com/nasaharvest/crop-cycle-detection): Crop-agnostic model that finds each cropping cycle in an NDVI time series and extracts green-up, peak, start and end of season. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: cropping cycles, phenology, NDVI, NASA Harvest, seasons
- [Satellite land surface temperature is not air temperature (LinkedIn post, TC Chakraborty)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): Argues that satellite LST (the "skin" temperature of roofs, roads and tree canopies) is far hotter than the air people feel, so using it to show heat hazard misleads the public and overstates how much trees cool the air. Replies discuss limited urban weather stations and when LST is still useful as a proxy. Main topic: [[Climate_Change]]. See also [[Urban_Planning]]. *Keywords*: land surface temperature, extreme heat, urban heat island, air temperature, science communication
- [Agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Open-source Python toolkit that delineates agricultural field boundaries from satellite/aerial imagery (Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS, NAIP) and embeddings (Google Satellite Embedding, TESSERA). Supports engines like Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, Prithvi and DINOv3, with SAM 2 refinement. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[Python]]. *Keywords*: field boundaries, delineation, satellite imagery, SAM, segmentation
- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Wherobots, Feb 2026) compares Earth embedding products (Clay, AlphaEarth, Tessera, Major TOM, Presto, Copernicus-Embed, Earth Index) by resolution, dimensions, license and storage cost. Argues they need shared standards: cloud-native formats, CRS metadata, provenance, benchmarks and quantization. See [[Embeddings]], [[Data]]. *Keywords*: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, GeoParquet, cloud-native, interoperability
