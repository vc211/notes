# Urban Planning

**Summary**: Notes on cities, urban heat, green infrastructure and planning for climate resilience. Related: [[Climate_Change]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

- [Nature-based Solutions for Disaster and Climate Resilience (SDG Academy, edX)](https://learning.edx.org/course/course-v1:SDGAcademyX+NBS001+3T2023a/block-v1:SDGAcademyX+NBS001+3T2023a+type@sequential+block@9dd4f68b2c134b428bd5f60): Online course from SDG Academy on using ecosystems (nature-based solutions) to reduce disaster risk and adapt to climate change. Main topic: [[Climate_Change]]. *Keywords*: nature-based solutions, disaster risk, adaptation, resilience, course
- [URBAL Sustainable Food Guide](https://urbal-sustainablefood.guide/): Free step-by-step method to map how sustainable food system innovations create change, with barriers, enablers and indicators. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. *Keywords*: food systems, sustainability, impact assessment, indicators, urban food
- [Global Surface UHI Explorer (Yale)](https://yceo.users.earthengine.app/view/uhimap): Earth Engine app to explore surface urban heat island intensity for cities worldwide. See [[Climate_Change]], [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. *Keywords*: urban heat island, surface temperature, cities, Earth Engine app
- [Satellite land surface temperature is not air temperature (LinkedIn post, TC Chakraborty)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): Argues that satellite LST (the "skin" temperature of roofs, roads and tree canopies) is far hotter than the air people feel, so using it to show heat hazard misleads the public and overstates how much trees cool the air. Replies discuss limited urban weather stations and when LST is still useful as a proxy. Main topic: [[Climate_Change]]. See also [[Remote_Sensing]]. *Keywords*: land surface temperature, extreme heat, urban heat island, air temperature, science communication
