# Soil

**Summary**: Notes on soil health, soil carbon, soil spectroscopy and soil data. Related: [[Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Global bare soil, photosynthetic and non-photosynthetic vegetation fraction, annual at 500 m](https://zenodo.org/records/12071317): Zenodo dataset of yearly global maps at 500 m showing the share of bare soil, green vegetation and dry (non-photosynthetic) vegetation per pixel. See [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Data]]. *Keywords*: fractional cover, bare soil, vegetation, global dataset, MODIS
- [Soil Spectroscopy for Global Good (GitHub)](https://github.com/soilspectroscopy): GitHub organization for the Open Soil Spectral Library (OSSL): data, calibration models, chemometrics tools and training on predicting soil properties from spectra. See [[Data]], [[Machine_Learning]]. *Keywords*: soil spectroscopy, OSSL, chemometrics, spectral library, soil properties
- [EU Soil Observatory Soil Degradation Dashboard (JRC)](https://esdac.jrc.ec.europa.eu/esdacviewer/euso-dashboard/): JRC dashboard mapping soil degradation in the EU; estimates about 62% of EU land is affected by one or more degradation processes. *Keywords*: soil health, soil degradation, EU, JRC, dashboard
- [Soil Carbon (Springer, Progress in Soil Science)](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-04084-4): Springer book (2014) collecting research on soil carbon: measurement, mapping, dynamics and management. See [[Climate_Change]]. *Keywords*: soil carbon, soil organic carbon, book, carbon sequestration
- [OpenLandMap Soil Platform (OpenGeoHub)](https://world.soils.app/olm-soildb/): OpenGeoHub platform to explore and access open soil data (SoilDB). See [[Data]]. *Keywords*: soil data, OpenLandMap, OpenGeoHub, soil database
