# Embeddings

**Summary**: Notes on vector embeddings, including Earth embeddings from geospatial foundation models. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]].
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [How Well Do Crop-Type Models Transfer with Embeddings? Exploring Generalization with AlphaEarth Embeddings](https://esmaeeladrah.com/2025/09/14/generalizing-crop-types-with-embedding/): Tests Random Forest crop classifiers on AlphaEarth embeddings across US, France and years; they transfer well, except wheat and barley across countries. Main topic: [[Agriculture]]. See also [[Machine_Learning]]. *Keywords*: AlphaEarth, crop type, transfer learning, Random Forest, embeddings
- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Wherobots, Feb 2026) compares Earth embedding products (Clay, AlphaEarth, Tessera, Major TOM, Presto, Copernicus-Embed, Earth Index) by resolution, dimensions, license and storage cost. Argues they need shared standards: cloud-native formats, CRS metadata, provenance, benchmarks and quantization. Main topic: [[Remote_Sensing]]. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords*: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, GeoParquet, cloud-native, interoperability
